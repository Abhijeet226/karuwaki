REGULATORY & DATA CUSTODY

Privacy & Compliance Framework

At KARUWAKISPEAKS.COM, reader sovereignty and transparent data stewardship form our operational bedrock. We minimize tracking, preserve your reading independence, and operate in strict alignment with regional cyber laws and international digital standards.

01

Data Privacy & Collection Policy

We only collect minimal telemetry and user-provided information essential to deliver requested services (such as account authorization, bookmark persistence, and editorial commentary).

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third-party data brokers. Any telemetry captured is anonymized and utilized solely to diagnose server load and optimize reading performance across our edge networks.

02

Offline Reading Vault & PWA Storage

Our Progressive Web App (PWA) and Offline Reading Vault leverage browser-sandboxed IndexedDB and CacheStorage APIs.

Articles, offline dispatches, and theme preferences cached locally on your hardware remain entirely under your device control. You can clear this stored data at any time via browser site settings or through your profile dashboard.

03

Regulatory Standards & Intermediary Guidelines

KARUWAKISPEAKS.COM adheres to applicable provisions of India's Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We respect the safe-harbor standards governing digital cultural archives and community publishing platforms.

For grievance redressal, copyright notices, or data removal requests, our designated compliance desk can be reached directly at admin@karuwakispeaks.com.

04

Editorial Independence & Content Disclaimer

Dispatches, commentary, and cultural essays published by guest scholars, contributors, and columnists express their individual research perspectives. Karuwaki Speaks fosters an open forum for intellectual, historical, and philosophical inquiry.
